About

A controls specialist, not an installer working to someone else's design.

Most BMS packages are split three ways: one firm pulls the cable, another configures the controllers, a third commissions the system. On a data centre, every handover between them is a place for errors to hide.

MM Tech delivers all three. Our engineers install the containment and field wiring, configure the controllers and graphics, then test and commission against the points schedule. One team owns the result, and the documentation reflects what was actually built.

Approach

Why contractors bring us in on mission-critical work.

The working habits a critical-environment programme needs from its controls partner.

On a data centre, a controls fault is not an inconvenience. It is a cooling alarm in a live hall. We plan, wire and record every job as if someone will audit it later, because on these sites someone usually does.

A

We work from the points schedule

Engineers read the control philosophy and points schedule directly, so what gets wired matches what was designed.

B

Every point is recorded

Terminations, test results and as-built changes are logged as the job progresses, not reconstructed at the end.

C

We follow the main contractor's programme

Sequencing is agreed with the main contractor and M&E consultant, and we flag clashes early.

D

Site discipline is the baseline

Permits, security clearances and live-environment procedures are part of the job from day one.

Process

How a project runs.

The same sequence on every job, fitted inside the wider construction programme.

Stage 1

Survey & scoping

Review drawings, points schedule and control philosophy. Agree scope and programme.

Stage 2

Containment & first fix

Install containment and field cabling along the agreed routes.

Stage 3

Panels & termination

Wire control panels and terminate field devices, recording every point.

Stage 4

Configure & commission

Configure controllers, then test and commission against the design intent.

Stage 5

Handover

As-built drawings, test records and O&M documentation delivered with the system.
