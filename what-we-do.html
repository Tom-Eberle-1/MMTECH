What we do

BMS and EPMS installation, delivered as one package.

We install building management and electrical power monitoring systems for hyperscale data centres and commercial buildings. One team takes the installation from first fix to a tested system, ready for commissioning.

Talk to us →What's in the package

BMS installation

Building management systems for critical environments.

We install the BMS across the mechanical plant, white space and support areas of hyperscale and colocation data centres, working to the controls specialist's drawings, points schedule and the main contractor's programme.

On a data centre, the BMS has hundreds of points to install, terminate and prove before commissioning can start, and the programme rarely has room for rework. Our job is to make sure it doesn't need any.

  • Field device installation [confirm]
  • Control panel installation and terminations [confirm]
  • Installed to the points schedule and drawings
  • Tested and recorded before handover

EPMS installation

Electrical power monitoring, from the same team.

We install the EPMS across the electrical infrastructure, alongside the BMS. Few installers cover both. Because we do, the two systems share one programme, coordinated containment routes and one point of contact.

For the main contractor, that means one less package to manage, one less set of interfaces to chase, and one team accountable for both installations.

  • Metering and monitoring device installation [confirm]
  • Communications cabling to the monitoring network [confirm]
  • Coordinated with the BMS installation and programme
  • Tested and recorded before handover

The package

The whole installation, from one team.

BMS and EPMS installations are often split between trades. Every handover between them is a place for delays and errors. We take the installation as a single package.

What we deliver

  • Primary and secondary containment
  • Field and communications cabling
  • Field device and panel installation
  • Terminations
  • Testing, with recorded results
  • Handover documentation [confirm]

Who we work alongside

Controller configuration, graphics and commissioning are carried out by the controls specialist. We install to their design and hand over a tested installation, so commissioning can start on programme.

We report to the main contractor or M&E contractor, and coordinate routes and access with the other trades on site.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you install containment?

Yes. Primary and secondary containment is undertaken as part of the package, so the installation isn't waiting on another trade before cabling can start.

Do you commission the system?

No. Controller configuration, graphics and commissioning are carried out by the controls specialist. We install to their design and hand over a tested installation that's ready for commissioning.

Can you take EPMS without the BMS, or the other way round?

Yes [confirm]. We can deliver either system on its own, though taking both gives you one team, one programme and one point of contact.

Do you work outside the UK?

Yes. We deliver projects across the UK and internationally, including in [countries].

What do you need to price a package?

The drawings, points schedule, specification and programme, plus your tender return date. If you only have part of that, send what you have and we'll tell you what else we need.

Do you take on commercial projects?

Yes. Data centres are our core work, but we also deliver BMS installations on commercial buildings, working alongside the M&E contractor.
