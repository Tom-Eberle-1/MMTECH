Terminations, testing & commissioning

Panel and field terminations to schematic, every core ferruled against the point schedule. Point-to-point testing recorded before energisation, then attendance through commissioning and witnessing to sign-off.

  • Every core identified to the points list
  • P2P and continuity records issued
  • Witnessed demos with the systems integrator
