Fig. 01 — About

Precision electrical engineering, applied where it matters most.

MM TECH Electrical Solutions is built around a single focus: becoming the recognised specialist in Building Management Systems and control wiring for hyperscale data centres. That focus shapes everything — how projects are scoped, how teams are trained, and how work is delivered on site.

We work primarily with main and tier one contractors, developers and consultants who need a partner capable of operating inside live, mission-critical environments — where documentation, sequencing and quality control carry as much weight as the wiring itself.

Fig. 02 — Core Expertise

Four disciplines. One standard of execution.

Building Management Systems

Design, installation and commissioning of BMS platforms that give operators accurate, real-time control over critical building infrastructure — from field devices through to head-end supervisory systems.

Control Wiring

Containment, termination and testing of control circuits to exacting specification, with full documentation and traceability at every stage — built for environments where fault-finding must be fast and certain.

Electrical Installations

Full electrical installation works delivered to current regulations and client-specific standards, coordinated tightly with M&E teams and site programmes on complex, multi-trade builds.

Data Centre Projects

End-to-end delivery inside hyperscale and enterprise data centre environments, where sequencing, security clearances and zero-disruption working are treated as part of the specification, not an afterthought.
